**Recommendation:** **Approve with changes** — the design is fundamentally sound and directly addresses the predecessor's confirmed defects, but contains **several major gaps** that, if left unfilled, would allow recurrence of L1–L10 or create new failure modes. ## 2. Benchmark Design (§6)

### 2.1 Label Classes (Ground Truth)

| Class | Well-Posed? | Distinguishable? | Credibility Risk | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DERIVED (natural)** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Low | — | Documented forks/ports with recorded lineage edges are credible. |
| **DERIVED (constructed)** | ⚠️ Partial | ⚠️ Partial | **[major]** | **Circularity risk.** The *same* transform pipeline that generates the constructed derivatives also defines the "depth" label used in RQ4. If the pipeline's notion of "depth" leaks into the metric design, the dose-response test is circular. **Fix:** Depth labels must be generated by an *independent* process (e.g., human expert rating or separate LLM-based depth estimation) and validated against the pipeline's output. |
| **EVOLVED** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Low | — | Adjacent releases are a credible baseline for conventional human evolution. |
| **INDEPENDENT** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | **[minor]** | **Selection bias risk.** "Clean-room implementations of the same specification" may converge on similar structures *by design*, making them poor true negatives for structural metrics. **Fix:** Must include *multiple* independent implementations per spec AND report per-spec results separately. |
| **UNRELATED** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Low | — | Different domains provide a clear null floor. |

**Finding:** The **constructed-derivative depth labeling is circular** — the transform pipeline that creates the derivatives also defines their depth, and the same pipeline's outputs are used to validate the depth metric. This violates **construct validity** for RQ4.

### 2.2 Family Coverage & Selection

- **≥8 families, ≥40 pairs** is a **floor, not a target**. The spec does not justify why 8 families or 40 pairs are *sufficient* for the claims.
- **Indicative families** are listed but **selection criteria are missing**. Without explicit criteria (e.g., "families must span ≥3 distinct domains", "each class must have ≥2 families"), the benchmark could be **gamed or unrepresentative**.
- **Natural vs constructed DERIVED ratio** is left as an **open decision point (§18.5)**. This is **dangerous** — if the ratio is too heavy on constructed derivatives, the benchmark may not reflect real-world derivation; if too heavy on natural, the depth labeling may be inconsistent.

**Severity:** **[blocker]** — Without explicit, pre-registered selection criteria for families and pairs, the benchmark lacks **construct validity** and **external validity**. The **open decision point (§18.5) must be resolved before pre-registration**.

### 2.3 Splits & Leakage Control

- **Family-level split** is **strong** and addresses leakage well.
- **Chardet v6/v7 held out as TEST** is correct.
- **Leave-one-family-out CV** on train for calibration is appropriate.

**Severity:** — (No issues here.)

### 2.4 Constructed-Derivative Pipeline

- **Documented, deterministic, seeded** — **good**.
- **Provenance banners + seed license retention** — **good**.
- **But:** The pipeline is **only described at a high level**. Critical details missing:
  - What *exact* transforms are applied at each depth level?
  - How is depth *validated* (e.g., human review, alternative tools)?
  - What *guarantees* that paraphrase depth 1 is "shallow" and re-architected depth 4 is "deep"?

**Severity:** **[major]** — Without operational details on the depth generations, **RQ4's dose-response test is unfalsifiable**. A reviewer cannot verify that the depth labels are meaningful.

## 3. Construct Validity (§5)

### 3.1 Target Construct Operationalization

**Claim:** "Derivation" is operationalized as a **latent ordinal**: `UNRELATED < INDEPENDENT < EVOLVED ≈ DERIVED-deep < DERIVED-moderate < DERIVED-paraphrase`.

**Problems:**

1. **Ordinal assumption is untested.** The spec *asserts* that EVOLVED ≈ DERIVED-deep, but provides no evidence or justification for this equivalence. If EVOLVED (conventional human evolution) is *not* equivalent to DERIVED-deep (AI-assisted deep rewrite), the ordinal scale is **invalid**.
   - **Severity:** **[major]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.1

2. **Latent vs observed.** The spec does not explain how the *latent* ordinal is **measured** by the three signal families (ST, BH, PB). Are ST/BH/PB *indicators* of the latent, or *definitional*? The circularity risk is high.
   - **Severity:** **[major]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.1

3. **Legal construct vs proxy.** The spec correctly states that CDA does not measure the *legal* construct, but the **proxy construct** (structural/behavioral retention) is **not fully operationalized**. What *exactly* does "structural retention" mean? How is it distinct from "behavioral retention"?
   - **Severity:** **[minor]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.1

### 3.2 Signal Families (ST, BH, PB)

| Family | Captures Derivation? | Scope-Parametric? | Invariance Claims | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ST (Structural retention)** | ⚠️ Partial | ✅ Yes | Renaming/reformatting | **[major]** | **Risk: confounded with domain shape.** Structural similarity may reflect *domain convergence* (e.g., all JSON parsers have similar AST shapes) rather than derivation. The spec acknowledges this in threats (§13) but does not **control for it** in the benchmark design. **Fix:** Must include *multiple* independent implementations per domain and show that ST discriminates DERIVED from INDEPENDENT *within* domains. |
| **BH (Behavioral equivalence)** | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ Partial | N/A (black-box) | **[minor]** | BH is **not scope-parametric** by construction (it measures whole-program behavior). The spec claims BH is "robust to source-level paraphrase" — true, but BH is *not* robust to **input-space coverage gaps**. If the workload is insufficient, BH may miss behavioral similarities. **Fix:** Must specify workload *coverage criteria* (e.g., "≥90% branch coverage" or "input corpus must trigger ≥X distinct code paths"). |
| **PB (Provenance & boundary)** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Dependency/import invariance | — | **Strong.** Mandatory multi-scope reporting fixes L1. |

**Finding:** **ST family risks measuring domain convergence, not derivation.** The spec's threats section acknowledges this but does not **mitigate it in the design**. Without controlling for domain, ST may fire on INDEPENDENT pairs that happen to have similar structures due to shared specifications.

### 3.3 Scope Contract (§5.3)

- **Mandatory ≥2 scopes (package + repo)** — **strong fix for L1**.
- **Scope-delta reporting** — **excellent diagnostic**.
- **Headline at package scope, repo as sensitivity** — **reasonable**.

**But:** The **definition of scope selectors** is **underspecified**:
- What constitutes a `package`? The spec says "importable shipped modules only" — but what about data files, type stubs, or generated code?
- What constitutes a `module`? Is it a Python module, a directory, or something else?
- `custom-glob` is "recorded verbatim" — but how is it **validated** for comparability?

**Severity:** **[minor]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.3

**Finding:** The scope contract is **operational** but **not fully specified**. Without precise definitions, researchers could **game the scope selection** to produce desired results.

### 4.2 Resampling & Multiplicity

- **BCa bootstrap CIs, ≥10k resamples, seeded** — **strong**.
- **Pair-level resampling** — **correct** (avoids pseudo-replication).
- **Holm-Bonferroni family-wise control** — **strong**.

**But:** The **comparison family is not enumerated** in §4 or §9. The spec says it will be "pre-declared" but does not **list the comparisons** here. Without the full family, **power cannot be calculated** and **multiplicity control is underspecified**.

**Severity:** **[major]** — SPECIFICATION.md:9

### 4.3 Power & Sample Size

- **Power calculation is mentioned** in §9.6 but **not specified**.
- **≥40 pairs / ≥8 families is a floor**, but **no justification** is given for why this is sufficient.
- **Small-n honesty** is addressed (rule-of-three intervals for n<power floor), but **the power floor itself is missing**.

**Severity:** **[blocker]** — SPECIFICATION.md:9.6

**Finding:** Without a **pre-registered power calculation**, the study risks being **underpowered**. The acceptance criteria (§17.4) require "test-set classifier performance with BCa CIs across ≥8 families", but if the test set is too small, the CIs will be **too wide to support the claims**.

### 4.4 Dose-Response (RQ4)

- **Spearman ρ between depth and score** — **circular** if depth labels are generated by the same process that the metrics are designed to detect.
- **No monotonicity test specified** beyond Spearman ρ. Should also test for **strict monotonicity** (e.g., Jonckheere-Terpstra) or **trend consistency**.

**Severity:** **[major]** — SPECIFICATION.md:4 (RQ4)

## 8. Scope Contract (§5.3 / §8)

### 8.1 Does Mandatory Multi-Scope Reporting Fix L1?

**Yes, but with caveats:**

- **L1 was:** Boundary metric scored 0.667 at repo-scope but 0.000 at package-scope, and the scope was **undisclosed**.
- **R-SCOPE fix:** Every metric must be reported at **≥2 scopes** (package + repo), with scope-delta shown.

**This is a strong fix** because:
1. Scope is **explicit and mandatory**.
2. Scope-delta is **published**, so confounds are visible.
3. Headline is at **package scope**, with repo as sensitivity.

**But:** The **choice of which scope is "headline"** is still a **researcher degree of freedom**. The spec says package scope is headline, but does not **justify why package scope is the "right" scope** for derivation disputes. Different disputes may involve different scopes (e.g., a dispute about a single module vs a whole repo).

**Severity:** **[minor]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.3

### 8.2 Edge Cases

- **What if a family has no `package` scope?** (e.g., a monorepo with no importable modules). The spec does not address this.
- **What if `custom-glob` scopes are incomparable?** The spec says they are "recorded verbatim" but does not ensure they are **semantically comparable**.

**Severity:** **[minor]** — SPECIFICATION.md:5.3

## 11. Explicit List of Blocking Issues

A reviewer **must not approve** this spec as-is because of the following **blockers**:

1. **[blocker]** **Benchmark selection criteria missing** (§6.2). Without explicit, pre-registered criteria for selecting families and pairs, the benchmark lacks **construct validity** and **external validity**. **Fix:** Add selection criteria (e.g., "families must span ≥3 domains", "each class must have ≥2 families") and freeze the final family list in pre-registration.
   - **Cites:** SPECIFICATION.md:6.2, 18.1

2. **[blocker]** **Power calculation missing** (§9.6). Without a pre-registered power calculation, the study risks being **underpowered**, and the AUC/ECE/Brier targets may be **unachievable**. **Fix:** Run and document a power calculation to set the **minimum pairs per class**, **test set size**, and **realistic CI widths** before pre-registration.
   - **Cites:** SPECIFICATION.md:9.6, 4 (RQ1–RQ3)

3. **[blocker]** **Depth labeling is circular** (§6.1, §6.4). The constructed-derivative pipeline defines both the derivatives *and* their depth labels, making RQ4's dose-response test **tautological**. **Fix:** Depth labels must be **independently generated and validated** (e.g., by human experts or a separate tool) before being used in confirmatory analysis.
   - **Cites:** SPECIFICATION.md:6.1, 6.4, 4 (RQ4)
